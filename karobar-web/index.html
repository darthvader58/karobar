Chrome extension for meticulous job tracking

Stop losing job applications and submissions between tabs, forms, and spreadsheets.

Karobar notices when you are on an application page, pulls the key details, and logs them into your Google Sheet before the trail goes cold.

Workflow

Three steps between finding a job and having it recorded cleanly.

Step 1

Open a job application page

Karobar checks the URL, page structure, and form signals to decide whether you are on a page worth logging.

Step 2

Review the prompt or scrape manually

Use the automatic prompt when detection fires, or trigger a scrape from the popup if you want control.

Step 3

Send the result to your Google Sheet

The extension writes the application details, checks for duplicates, and keeps recent activity visible in the popup.

Setup

The first-run path is short.

1. Sign in

Authenticate with Google so Karobar can write to your spreadsheet.

2. Paste a sheet URL or ID

Use any Google Sheet you want. The extension validates it before saving.

3. Start applying

Karobar handles the repetitive logging while you focus on applications.
